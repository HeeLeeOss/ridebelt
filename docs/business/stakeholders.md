# Stakeholder Plan v0.1 (Phase 7)

| Stakeholder | What they need to hear | What we need from them | Order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent ride or safety engineer | — | Gate 1 and Gate 2 reviews | 1 |
| Ride manufacturers (e.g., B&M, Intamin, RMC, Mack, Zamperla, S&S, Funtime) | RAS doesn't touch their restraint design or liability; it adds a low-cost differentiator | Seat geometry, load data, a pilot seat for fit trials | 2 |
| ASTM F24 committee | A gap exists for secondary rider-retention anchors | Feedback, and eventually a work item | 3 |
| Amusement insurers | Lower ejection-claim exposure | Premium-credit pilot | 3 |
| State ride-safety regulators | Supports inspections; doesn't weaken primary-restraint checks | Guidance on whether rider-supplied gear is allowed | 4 |
| Park operators | Optional; guest-satisfaction and safety story; free retrofit license | Pilot ride, operator procedures, evacuation drill | 4 |
| Riders and advocacy groups (e.g., incident victims' families) | Honest framing: a backup, not a guarantee | Feedback on comfort and acceptance | 5 |

## Messaging rules
- Never claim RideBelt "prevents deaths" or replaces any ride safety system.
- Always say it is **optional, used with park approval, and a backup only**.
- Be open about what it **can't** help with: structural failures, collisions.
