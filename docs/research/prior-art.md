# Prior Art & IP Search (Phase 0)

## Search plan
- **Databases:** Google Patents, USPTO PatFT/AppFT, Espacenet, WIPO Patentscope
- **CPC classes to start from:** A63G 31/00 (amusement arrangements, including ride restraints), A62B 35/00 (safety belts and harnesses), B60R 22 (vehicle safety belts), F16B 45 (hooks and carabiners)
- **Keywords:** "secondary restraint" amusement ride; "passenger tether" ride; "lap bar backup" restraint; "rider harness" coaster; "anchor point" ride seat
- **Product scan:** zip-line and ropes-course harness systems, aerial-lift and chairlift restraints, motorsport HANS/harness anchors, aviation lap-belt anchors, and child car-seat anchors (LATCH/ISOFIX, a key analogy for a universal anchor standard)

## Analogies worth studying
| System | Lesson for RideBelt |
|---|---|
| ISOFIX / LATCH (child seats) | A universal anchor standard that got into every new vehicle through regulation and voluntary adoption. **This is the model for RAS** |
| Continuous-belay zip-line systems | Rider-worn harness, operator-controlled release, and connectors that can't be opened by mistake |
| Fall-arrest lanyards | Energy absorbers, inspection and retirement rules |
| Motorsport harnesses | Multi-point restraint under high G, plus quick release for rescue |
