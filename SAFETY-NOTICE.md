# ⚠️ Safety Notice: DRAFT, not reviewed, not for use

**RideBelt is a concept-stage design project. Nothing in this repository has been reviewed or approved by a qualified, credentialed safety or ride engineer, and nothing has been tested or certified.**

- **Do not build, wear or use** any RideBelt design, or any device based on it, on an amusement ride or anywhere else.
- **Do not attach anything to an amusement ride** or its restraints. Doing so may interfere with the ride's engineered safety systems, and it may break park rules and the law.
- Loads, angles, strengths and procedures in this repository are **preliminary estimates** from simplified models and public sources. They may be wrong.
- Documents marked **DRAFT** have not passed the project's **MVP review gate** (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)). Only content marked **RELEASED**, with a recorded credentialed sign-off, may be relied on for anything. As of now, **no content is released.**
